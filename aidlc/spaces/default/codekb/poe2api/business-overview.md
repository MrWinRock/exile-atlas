# Business Overview

Exile Atlas is a browser workspace for Path of Exile 2 players who plan builds and maps, author loot filters, inspect items and research markets. Most planning and editing runs with local drafts. A connected GGG account enables character and filter operations.

## User Workflows

- Research an item via the item catalogue, trade draft, or Search Toolkit. The Search Toolkit compiles English in-game searches for waystones, tablets, relics, vendor items and equipment. It supports item-text checks, named presets and compatible trade-draft transfer.
- Select a completed UTC hour and league on Currency to compare a direct pair, browse item markets, or read collected price and stock history.
- Plan passive, Atlas and Genesis allocations, compare builds, work through crafting budgets, and assess map safety with purpose-built editors.

## Relevant Boundaries

The project ships versioned reference snapshots. Regex compilation and pasted-item tests approximate game search behavior. GGG exchange digests describe completed hours and report paired low/high quantities. The app currently presents directional price ranges and uses their midpoint for historical charts.
